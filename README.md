# Auction Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes auction pricing, sales performance, and revenue trends to identify opportunities for business growth.

## Business Objective

Analyze auction performance and identify factors that influence revenue generation and conversion rates.

## Analysis Areas

- Revenue Trends
- Pricing Analysis
- Conversion Rates
- Vehicle Category Performance
- Monthly Performance Tracking

## Tools Used

- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI

## Example KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Average Selling Price
- Conversion Rate
- Revenue by Vehicle Category
- Revenue Growth Rate

## Insights

- Top-performing auction categories
- Revenue growth opportunities
- Pricing optimization opportunities
